WORK ABOUT CONTACT
progress 01 — pigment & gesture studies

Early experiments with raw pigments and fluid dynamics. Each stroke was recorded and translated into a digital score, revealing hidden rhythms between hand and material.

progress 02 — organic algorithms & field notes

Lichen growth patterns and mineral deposits informed a generative drawing system. The studio log shows how algorithmic errors became intentional textures.

final result — «chromatic recursion»

The completed work merges analog brushwork with real-time feedback loops. The final stills capture the tension between control and chance.

Mixed media on canvas, 120 x 90 cm each panel. The diptych is part of an ongoing series exploring permeable boundaries.

The Artist

I'm Carole El Danaf, an artist and architect specializing in computational and generative design systems.

In my current research, KOMO, I focus on the algorithms of motion found in nature.
